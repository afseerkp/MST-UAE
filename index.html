Why Deira

Dubai still trades the old way here.

We could have taken an office in a tower. We chose Deira: a counter, a phone that gets answered, and a price you can ask about face to face.

Many offices have no IT department, just an office manager who also looks after the laptops and needs four of them by Sunday. We work for that person: one list, one quotation, one tax invoice, and someone to call when something fails.

Questions

Answers before you ask.

Anything not here, ask on WhatsApp and a person in the shop will answer.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do you sell to individuals, or only to companies?

Both. Anyone can buy a single laptop or a mouse at the counter; companies get itemised quotations, LPO ordering and, once set up, credit terms.

Do I get a VAT tax invoice?

Yes. Every sale comes with a tax invoice showing our TRN and 5% VAT, in your company’s name if you give it to us.

Are the licences genuine?

Yes. Windows, Microsoft 365, Adobe and the rest come through authorised channels and are invoiced to you. We don’t sell grey-market keys, however cheap they look online.

What warranty comes with hardware?

The manufacturer’s, usually one to three years depending on the model. If something fails, bring it in or call us, and we deal with the brand for you.

Do you deliver outside Dubai?

Yes, to all seven emirates. Dubai orders usually arrive the next working day; Abu Dhabi and the northern emirates a day or two later.

Can you install what you sell?

Yes: laptops set up with users and mail, printers on the network, cabling, Wi-Fi, CCTV and servers. Installation is its own line on the quotation, so you see what it costs.

How can we pay?

Cash, card or bank transfer. Companies with a credit account can pay by cheque or transfer on agreed terms.

Can you quote for a tender or a fit-out?

Yes. Send the BOQ or the specification and we quote line by line, with a delivery schedule if the project runs in stages.

Will you match another quotation?

Send it. If we can match it on the same genuine product, we will; if we can’t, we’ll say so plainly.

Do you repair laptops?

Yes, the common repairs and upgrades: screens, keyboards, batteries, SSD and RAM. You get the cost before we start.

Do you offer annual maintenance?

Yes. An AMC covers scheduled visits, priority call-outs and remote support for a fixed yearly fee, sized to your number of users.

When are you open?

Saturday to Thursday, 9 am to 9 pm, and Friday from 4 pm to 9 pm. WhatsApp messages are answered in opening hours.
